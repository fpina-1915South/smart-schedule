How to use this

1Find your hot zonesOrange hours are the busiest of each day. That's where the team needs to be.
2Use the standard shiftsEvery shift starts 30 minutes early for the huddle. Pick Opener, Mid or Closer for each day.
3Hit your hoursFull-timers land at 40, part-timers around 20. Hours total on the right, PTO included. Red means under or over.
4Check and postRed hours are short, blue hours are heavy. Fix them, then post. Until you post, the week says Draft in orange and your team can't see it.

Your week at a glance

Hot zone. 75% or more of that day's busiest hour. All hands on the floor, leader included.
Building. 50 to 75% of peak. Steady traffic, staff it solidly.
Lighter. Breaks, lunches, follow-ups and delivery calls go here.

Build the week

Click a name to edit it, pick a role, rate each seller High, Middle or Low, then pick a shift for each day. Blue is an Opener, green is a Closer, orange is a Mid, purple is Sunday, teal is Part-time, gray dashed is PTO. Every shift of 6 hours or more gets a lunch time under it. The tool staggers lunches into your lightest hours, and you can change any of them. Names carry over from your last saved week.

Your team

Each box starts at the recommended number for 1-to-1 coverage with 40-hour weeks. Change it to who you actually have and the tool shows whether you're staffed right and fills your busiest hours first. Part-timers count as half a consultant, so two part-timers equal one full-timer. New hires are added on the schedule with + Add new hire. They show on the schedule but don't count toward coverage or staffing until their training weeks are up, then they count automatically. Names you've typed are kept.

Coach's corner

Schedule scoreboard

Every store's schedule for the week, graded the same way. Click a store to open it.

How the grade works (100 points): guest coverage 35 (hot-zone hours count double) · leader in the store 20 · talent mix 15 (a High in every hot zone, Lows never outnumber Highs and Middles, new hires always with a High or Middle, team rated) · hours targets 10 · posted before the week starts 10 · right hours 10 (no heavy hours while other hours are short). Any hour with no leader in the store caps the grade at a C. A 90+, B 80+, C 70+, D 60+, F below 60.

Actual traffic: validate the week

Upload what really walked in from ShopperTrak or eTrax. Every store's week is then graded again against real traffic, and the forecast is checked against what happened.

One row per store, date and hour: store,date,hour,guests (for example Baton Rouge,2026-10-05,14,17). A wide file with one column per hour (store,date,10,11,12,...) works too. eTrax counts are one per guest, so they're used as they are. Rows for the same store, date and hour are added together.

Staffing standard

Holidays and tentpoles

Closed every year

Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and Easter. No shifts can be scheduled.

Tentpoles every year

Presidents Day, Memorial Day and Labor Day weekends (Saturday to Monday) and Black Friday (Friday to Monday). They repeat automatically. Click Change to set different hours for one date.

Custom tentpoles and changes

Set fixed hours, or open early and keep each store's normal close. Add store exceptions where one store runs different hours. Traffic lift multiplies the normal traffic for that day so staffing scales up (1 = normal day, 2 = double).
